I think being untouched by others is pure. That purity is precious. But because it is pure, you cannot ignore the lack of experience. I am not talking about sexual skill. I am talking about understanding and knowledge of individual human beings.

What kinds of people exist in this world, what kind of lives they live, what kind of environments they grew up in, what kind of education they received. These are things you naturally learn through relationships.

That said, let me say this first. I am not saying that virgins necessarily lack social skills or only have limited relationships. And I am not saying that non-virgins necessarily have social skills and fulfilling relationships.

But those who praise virgins and look down on non-virgins should ask themselves whether they really know the reality of virgins.

Imagine this. Suppose a virgin is a virgin because their relationships with people have been sparse, and because of that sparseness their knowledge is lacking.

A person’s words and actions are strongly shaped by the environment they grew up in and the education they received. A frog in a well.

That person might carelessly snap at what you believe is right, or make unnecessary remarks about how you live. If that continues, it may slowly build up as stress without you noticing.

If you can still love that person and grow together, I have nothing to say. But if it becomes unmanageable, I would tell you to stop.

In the debate over whether virgins or non-virgins are better, I think people who fully affirm virgins tend to see them only as an ideal image.

Don’t just eat the convenient parts. Virgins are troublesome too. Loving them including that troublesome side is the real virgin enthusiast. Don’t be a fashion virgin enthusiast.

I prefer virgins!
