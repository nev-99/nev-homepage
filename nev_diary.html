Nev's Diary
This is a scripted excerpt from the high school diary of "Nev". The entries are presented in the order they were originally written, but the collection remains incomplete as his belongings are still being sorted.
# 2014.3.23 finally found out the thing i was dreading. yeah… the girl i like took a two-shot pic w/ some other dude. they looked hella happy. like, she never did that kinda stuff when she was w/ me. damn… i should’ve just confessed back then. should’ve been a bit more real, more honest. if i had, then even if this happened, i could at least blame her for cheating on me. but nah, i was too shy, so now i can’t even show legit anger over this “already done deal” shit… why do i always screw up when it actually matters? she’s gonna be gone from here for a while… and i didn’t even get to see her one last time. instead, i’m stuck w/ this garbage pic burned into my brain as the final memory. how does it get this bad? i don’t even get to send her off… just gotta stay home, quiet and empty like some loser. this sucks. seriously… this sucks. shit, shit, shit me n her didn’t exactly meet in some clean, legit way. i was at this party some other dude dragged me to, and yeah… i fell for her at first sight. but ofc i couldn’t even go talk to her myself. froze up like always. but then—she was the one who showed interest in me. we ended up swapping contacts after that. one day, she asked me out. like, straight up invited me on a date. but me being me, i was kinda messed up back then… and then i caught a cold a few days before, and canceled last minute the day before. at that point i was like, yeah, it’s over. i blew it. but somehow, later on, we got another chance and actually went on that date. we were both awkward as hell, not gonna lie, but… it kinda felt like we were on the same wavelength. to keep it short, that date was “good” right before the ticket gates, she waved at me like she didn’t wanna leave… yeah, i swear i saw it. after that, we kept texting for a few days straight. but then… her replies started getting worse. slower, drier. i figured she was just busy, so for the last exchange i just reacted and left it at that. maybe that was a mistake too. i texted her again later and we started talking again, but then she told me she was moving away. so yeah, i wanted to see her one more time. but she seemed busy getting ready to move, so i didn’t wanna push it and ask her out again. still thought maybe—just maybe—there was a chance, so i asked, “u got plans for the next few days already?” she said, “yeah” and then… i checked her profile. and that pic showed up right in front of me. so those “plans” weren’t moving prep or anything like that. it was time she was spending with someone else. and now… i’ve got all this crap bottled up and nowhere to put it. so yeah… i’m just dumping it here like this, pathetic as hell… but yeah… i get it. i’ve done so much shitty stuff up till now that this kinda thing? it’s barely even the beginning. i’m pathetic. straight up beyond saving. no way out either. and i’m living w/ that in mind… like, i know what i am. plus, we weren’t even dating. so wherever she goes, whoever she’s w/, whatever she does—it’s all her call. same goes for me. but still… why does it feel like this… damn… this is just way too much. no music, no words—nothing can save me. not like i even deserve to be saved in the first place. there’s nothing there for a guy like me. and if i tried telling someone all this— anyone who actually knows me would just go, “well, yeah, obviously” and brush it off in a second. that’s why it feels so empty. nothing i can do. nowhere to put this. i’m even criticizing myself, so where the hell am i supposed to throw all this at? if i try to show myself as i really am, it’s just full of contradictions. to anyone “decent” i’m just trash. so instead of understanding, all i’d get back is insults. i can’t even talk about myself properly unless i split myself into different versions, like they’re separate people. that kind of cowardice? yeah, it’s just dishonest. so i end up saying nothing. i’m not even supposed to say anything in the first place… i’m me… and at the same time, not me
He disappeared in late 2025. I took his place and became "NEV". It was what he wanted. And now, we have become one. Forever.
